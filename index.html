About Us

Advanced Care Solutions In
Chiropractic Care

When you seek care from any healthcare professional, it's beneficial when that person not only knows what they are talking about but has experienced it themselves. Dr. Edward Hevner of Hevner Chiropractic Center in Lititz, PA, is a passionate provider in part because he has been receiving chiropractic care since childhood. He knows just how powerful it can be for relieving pain and keeping patients at the top of their game.

Mission

To provide evidence-based, drug-free chiropractic care focused on recovery, lasting comfort, and optimal physical well-being for all patients.

Vision

To be a trusted provider of individualized spinal care that helps individuals move better, eliminate pain, and live with vitality at every stage of life.

Expert Provider

Highly experienced practitioner specializing in Gonstead adjustments, Cox decompression, PNF stretching, and full-spine biomechanics.

Personalized Plans

No cookie-cutter routines and no long-term upfront treatment plans. Treatments are designed strictly around your specific condition and goals.

Measurable Results

Our clinical goal is to help you achieve a 30% to 50% improvement in symptoms within the very first 2 weeks of dedicated care.

Holistic & Referrals

We facilitate outside referrals when appropriate for MRI/imaging, pain management, surgical consult, and physical therapy. The patient's health always comes first.
